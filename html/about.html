A BIT ABOUT US

Engineering Precision.
Architectural Vision.

Architectural and Civil Engineering Services (ACES), established in 2003, is a premier multidisciplinary engineering consultancy serving public and private sector visionaries across Pakistan.

With a permanent team of over 100 seasoned engineers, urban planners, and architects backed by ISO 9001 certification, ACES delivers holistic end-to-end solutions—from initial master planning and geotechnical survey to advanced structural modeling, sustainable MEP networks, and resident project supervision.
